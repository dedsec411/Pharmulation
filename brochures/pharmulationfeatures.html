Artificial intelligence Five systems

01

Prescription Lens

Photograph any prescription, medication label, discharge summary or inpatient chart and it becomes a playable case. Every medicine read off the page is resolved against the live catalogue first, so the case is always playable. The patient name is replaced on the server before anything is displayed or stored, the image is never saved, and a read the model is not confident in is refused rather than guessed.

02

AI Clinical Reasoning Examiner

A viva that opens automatically once a case is scored and cannot be dismissed. Three questions about the decisions just made, answered in the learner's own words and marked on four axes. Three examiner personalities: Dr. Hassan (strict), Dr. Hakim (Socratic), Dr. Zara (practical).

03

AI patient

The OTC consultation is a typed conversation across the counter. The patient improvises its wording but cannot invent facts beyond the case, and will withhold relevant history until asked. Marked against the WWHAM framework.

04

Dr. Hakim, AI mentor

An in-app tutor for medicines, doses, counselling, interactions, calculations, compounding and study help, available alongside gameplay.

05

Weekly progress report

An AI-written summary of the week's practice, generated at most once per learner per week and surfaced on the dashboard.

Learning and analytics

  • Predictive weakness heatmap — seven clinical skills across every drug class, built from the error log. A cell with too little behind it says so rather than showing a figure built on two data points.
  • Recommended cases — today's practice chosen from that map, offered alongside the mode cards rather than replacing them.
  • Peer benchmarking — where a learner stands against others at the same rank, computed server-side and returning nothing about anyone else.
  • Error explanation panel — why each mistake was a mistake, at the moment it is made.
  • Failure debrief — a structured review when a case is lost.
  • Case file slides — the clinical picture read one slide at a time, the way a handover actually runs.
  • Simulated prescription viewer, mentor tips during play, and a case celebration screen on completion.

Faculty platform

  • Classes with join codes — faculty is a role chosen at signup, with no administrator in the loop.
  • Cohort overview — students enrolled, cases completed this week, students gone quiet, assignments past due.
  • Assignments with deadlines, shown on the student's own dashboard.
  • Timed assessments with a managed sitting: the clock bar follows the student across the whole app, not just the game screen.
  • Class analytics and per-student drill-down, including the weakness map read across a cohort.
  • Row-level security isolation — an educator can only ever read students enrolled in their own classes, and a student only their own. Enforced in the database, not the interface.

Engineering and integrity

  • Row-level security on every table; the AI key never reaches the browser.
  • XP cannot be farmed — it is bound at the database level to a case that was genuinely recorded, and each case can be claimed exactly once.
  • Rate limits and sanity constraints on recorded scores.
  • 273 automated tests across 16 files.
  • React 19, TanStack Start with SSR, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase (Postgres, auth, RLS) and Google Gemini.
