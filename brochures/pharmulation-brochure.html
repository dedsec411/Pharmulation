The problem

Practice judgement, not recall.

Written assessment measures what a student can recall. The counter measures what they decide — with an interaction in front of them and a queue behind. Pharmulation puts the learner in the job and marks every decision: the medicine, the interaction, the label, the counselling.

Flagship feature

Prescription Lens — photograph it, play it.

2.7–7.5 s
measured, real photos

Photograph a handwritten prescription, a medication label, a discharge summary or an inpatient chart, and it becomes a playable case in seconds. Every medicine read off the page is matched against the live catalogue first, so the case is always playable.

The name never lands. Replaced with a fictional one on the server, before anything is shown or stored.
The image is never stored. Held in memory for one request and discarded. No bucket, and no id to fetch it by.
An unclear read is refused. Below the confidence threshold it asks for a better photo rather than inventing a case.
Four practice environments

Community Pharmacy

Dispense from the shelf, catch the interaction, then build the label: frequency, timing, duration, instruction. OTC is a typed consultation with an AI patient, marked against WWHAM.

Clinical

Build medication orders on the ward: dose, route and frequency, interaction checks, and renal adjustment.

Industry

Run a tablet batch from formula to release — batch booklet, in-process checks, QA release.

Warehousing

Goods receiving, FEFO stock rotation, cold-chain integrity and stock reconciliation.

How it marks you

AI Clinical Reasoning Examiner

A case shows what a learner did, not whether they knew why. After each one: three questions on the decisions just made, answered in their own words and marked on four axes rather than right or wrong. Pick your examiner — Dr. Hassan accepts no hedging, Dr. Hakim is Socratic, Dr. Zara asks only what you would do at the counter.

Predictive weakness analytics

Every mistake is logged against a drug class and a clinical skill, building a heatmap across seven skills. Where a cell has too little behind it, it says so rather than showing a figure built on two data points. The map then drives recommended cases.

Progression and CPD

Six ranks, plus badges, streaks and peer benchmarking. One CPD hour per ten completed cases, with downloadable certificates at 1, 5, 10, 25 and 50 hours.

For faculty

Run a cohort, not a spreadsheet

  • Classes with join codes. Faculty is a role chosen at signup — no administrator in the loop.
  • Cohort overview: students enrolled, cases this week, students gone quiet, assignments past due.
  • Assignments and timed assessments with managed sittings.
  • Class analytics with per-student drill-down — the weakness map, read across a cohort.

Isolation enforced by the database

Not by the interface. Row-level security means an educator can only read data for students in their own classes, and a student only their own — a guarantee that holds whatever the front end asks for.

Progression that cannot be gamed

XP is bound at the database level to a case that was genuinely recorded and can be claimed exactly once, so a cohort's figures mean what they say.
